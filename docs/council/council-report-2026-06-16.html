BFG Onboarding Architecture — Council Verdict

Five advisors · anonymous peer review · chairman synthesis — 2026-06-16
The question: Destroy the current BFG onboarding as a unified system — Signup → Seed Form → Goal → Sex → Level → Environment → Schedule → Building Your Path → Avatar Formation → Naming Ceremony → Home — judged on speed, clarity, emotional connection, long-term retention, and fit with BFG's own philosophy. Verdict A (keep) / B (partial) / C (rebuild).

Chairman Verdict

C — REBUILD THE SEQUENCE

Rebuild the onboarding architecture — but preserve the Presence concept. The flow isn't slightly off; it is unified around the exact failure its own foundational doc names as fatal. It opens with Goal — a named Day-0 anti-pattern — then stacks three identity ceremonies (Seed Form, Avatar Formation, Naming) onto a shame-carrying user who came to work out, not to be auditioned. Nearly every step moves, merges, or relocates. That is a rebuild, not a tweak. What you do not rebuild is the Unified Presence idea itself — that's the moat.

Where the council agreed (high confidence)

  • Goal must leave Day 0. All five flagged it. It violates BFG's own anti-pattern, earns nothing toward the first workout, and forces a fragile user to declare who they want to be before they've done a rep.
  • Naming + Avatar Formation belong AFTER the first workout, as earned payoffs. Naming an unearned blob is hollow — and is the literal moment the Outsider put the phone down ("christening a Tamagotchi alone in my kitchen").
  • The Day-0 job is logistics, not identity. Only Environment, Schedule, Level (with Sex merged) earn their place before the first session.
  • "Present, not Performing" is currently violated. You cannot claim a witnessing Presence and then run a formation ceremony — that is pure performance.

Where the council clashed

The Contrarian, Executor and Outsider wanted to strip Day 0 to bare logistics. The Expansionist argued the Presence "imprinting" moment is the entire moat and must not be diluted. Peer review judged the Expansionist's blind spot real: it designs Day 90 for a user who never finishes Day 0 — but its core point stands.

The blind spot peer review caught (the synthesis nobody said alone)

Deferring everything quietly guts the Presence concept. If Presence only shows up after the workout, you lose the imprinting the Expansionist correctly calls the asset. The resolution: Presence is there from second zero, but un-ceremonied. It silently witnesses the logistical questions (asks nothing about identity), and the Avatar + Name emerge earned from the first completed session — authored by behavior, not demanded upfront. That keeps the lean Day-0 gate AND the "I've been here since before your first rep" payoff.

Also flagged: nobody proposed measurement. No D+1 return metric, no A/B (full ceremony vs. lean), no kill criterion, no check that the shame-carrying persona actually dominates signups. Decide this with data, not intuition.

The one thing to do first

Pull Goal out of Day 0 and move the Naming Ceremony to after the first completed workout. Those two moves alone remove the named anti-pattern and the documented checkout point.

Recommended structure

Day 0 — logistics only, Presence present but silent:

Signup→ Presence appears (neutral, witnesses, asks nothing)→ Environment→ Schedule→ Level + Sex (one screen)→ Home · first session queued

Earned — after the first workout completes:

Avatar takes form (Seed becomes theirs)→ Naming→ Goal emerges (Presence earns the right to ask)

Keep / Change / Delete

KEEPUnified Presence concept (the moat) · Environment, Schedule, Level as the Day-0 core · Seed Form as the origin of the avatar (co-authorship) · "Present, not Performing" — now actually honored
CHANGEGoal: first question → deferred, asked by Presence after session one · Sex: own screen → merged into Level · Presence Day 0: reveal/ceremony → silent witness hosting logistics · Jargon ("Presence / Path / Avatar / Ceremony") → plain language
DELETESeed Form as a standalone staring gate (fold into flow) · "Building Your Path" loading-screen-as-meaning · the triple-ceremony stack on Day 0

Most risky decisions (council-flagged)

1Opening with Goal. Violates BFG's own named Day-0 anti-pattern. Highest-confidence risk.
2Naming before the first workout. The Outsider's exact checkout point — manufactures the "feeling stupid" the product is meant to prevent.
3Presence as "embodiment of progression." Makes the witness a scoreboard that also embodies failure — a living mascot of backsliding waiting at Home for the lapsing user. A product-level risk beyond onboarding.
4Identical-for-all Seed Form + "meeting you" claim. Same blob for everyone undercuts the personalization the claim promises.
5No measurement. No activation metric, no A/B, no kill criterion, no persona validation. Shipping conviction without evidence.

The five advisors

"A wedding for someone terrified of marriage." Three escalating identity contracts before one rep; the witness becomes a scoreboard of backsliding.

Wrong problem. Real job: survive session one without being asked to become someone. "What's the least we can ask before we let them in?"

Peer review highlights

Strongest response: split between the Contrarian (best diagnosis — the doctrinal contradiction: a witness made into an embodiment of progress must also embody failure) and the First Principles Thinker (best reframe + generative principle).

Biggest blind spot: the Expansionist — builds a 365-day attachment thesis on a Day 0 the fragile user never finishes.

What all missed: (1) deferring everything breaks the Presence concept — resolved by the "present but un-ceremonied, naming emerges earned" synthesis; (2) no one proposed measuring it (D+1 return, A/B full-vs-lean, kill criteria, persona validation); (3) consider making the ceremony skippable rather than mandatory or absent.
