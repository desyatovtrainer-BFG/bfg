# BFG — Onboarding Architecture Stress Test · Council Transcript

Date: 2026-06-16
Method: LLM Council (5 independent advisors → anonymized peer review → chairman synthesis)
Grounded against: `docs/BFG_BEGINNER_JOURNEY.md`

---

## Original question

Destroy the BFG onboarding as a unified system. Current architecture:

`Signup → Seed Form → Goal → Sex → Level → Environment → Schedule → Building Your Path → Avatar Formation → Naming Ceremony → Home`

Judge on speed, clarity, emotional connection, long-term retention, fit with BFG's own philosophy. Verdict A (keep) / B (partial) / C (rebuild). Then show recommended structure, keep/change/delete, and most risky decisions.

## Framed question (key enrichment)

BFG = long-term transformation product, Unified Presence (Avatar=Body, Companion=Voice, one entity). **Critical internal conflict surfaced from the project's own foundational doc `BFG_BEGINNER_JOURNEY.md`:** target user has started/stopped before, carries shame, fears looking stupid. The doc explicitly says *"Do NOT ask them to set a goal on Day 0 — a threat disguised as a feature,"* names *"Goal-setting prompts on Day 0"* as an anti-pattern, mandates logistical not identity questions, and states the companion *"witnesses, does not cheer"* and *"does not have goals for the user."* Yet the current onboarding opens with Goal and stacks three identity ceremonies.

---

## Advisor responses

### The Contrarian
Onboarding is unified around the exact failure its own doc names as fatal. Opens with Goal (the named anti-pattern), then doubles down: Seed Form, Avatar Formation, Naming Ceremony — three escalating identity contracts before one rep. The shame-carrying user doesn't feel met, they feel auditioned. Deepest contradiction: the companion "witnesses, does not have goals," yet step two extracts a goal and Presence is the "embodiment of progression" — you made the witness a scoreboard that can also embody failure, a living mascot of backsliding waiting at Home. Churn engine. Seed Form and Avatar Formation are the same "becoming" beat told twice; three ceremonies fatigue rather than stack. Sex/Level/Environment/Schedule buried mid-ceremony read as a medical intake smuggled inside a baptism. Fatal flaw: the system optimizes for the *meaning of beginning* while the user's actual fear is *continuing*. "You're throwing a wedding for someone terrified of marriage."

### The First Principles Thinker
The team is solving the wrong problem, and the conflict in its own docs proves it. Strip it down: the first session's real job for a shame-carrying restarter is to **survive without being asked to become someone**; the win condition is returning tomorrow. Onboarding right now is an identity gauntlet dressed as a greeting. Avatar Formation and Naming Ceremony are Goal-on-Day-0's threat amplified — asking someone who isn't sure they belong to commit to a permanent embodied identity in minute three. Ceremony raises stakes; this user needs stakes lowered. You can't claim "Present, not Performing" and then run a formation ceremony — that's pure performance. Reframe: onboarding is where Presence demonstrates it won't demand anything. Replace identity questions with logistical ones; let Presence arrive already-formed and neutral; let naming and goals emerge later, earned through showing up. The right question isn't "what's the best onboarding order?" — it's "what's the least we can ask before we let them in?"

### The Expansionist
The Presence-meeting-you inversion is the most undervalued asset BFG has, and the team treats it as a flourish when it's the entire moat. Every fitness product competes on features; BFG could be the only one competing on attachment — and attachment doesn't churn. The "no goals, just witness" philosophy isn't a constraint to tolerate, it's the differentiator. Reframe Day 0 as *imprinting*: the Presence doesn't ask "what do you want to become" (threat); it says "I'm here now, and I'll remember everything." The most powerful retention asset in the category is a companion who can later say "I've been with you since the beginning" — impossible for any competitor who started with a goal form. Under-exploited: **memory as the product** (Day 90/365 callbacks), and the **seed form as a co-authored "we grew this together" artifact** — co-authorship beats customization for attachment. Move Goal out of Day 0 entirely; let it emerge once the Presence has earned the right to ask. Onboarding that ends in a relationship, not a profile, is a category of one.

### The Outsider
I downloaded a workout app. I want to work out. Instead I'm shown a glowing blob with no face that calls itself "a symbol of the beginning of my path." I didn't ask for a path. The interview — goal, sex, level, where, schedule — I'd tolerate; that's a normal app figuring out what to give me. But then "Building Your Path," then an "Avatar," then a "Naming Ceremony" where I name the blob. That's where I put the phone down. I came here sweaty and a little embarrassed, and now I'm christening a virtual pet like a Tamagotchi, alone in my kitchen — the exact "stupid" feeling I was told I'd be spared. Words that mean nothing: "Presence," "Path," "Avatar." "Meeting a Presence" does not land — it's the same blob everyone got, so it's clearly not about me. Every screen between sign-up and first workout is the app talking about itself. Let me work out first. Earn the blob. Don't make me name anything before I've sweated once.

### The Executor
Of ~10 steps, three earn Monday morning: **Environment** (is a workout even possible), **Schedule** (anchors the session to a real slot), **Level** (prevents a humiliating overshoot). Everything else is friction or ceremony that pays off later, not Day 0.
- **Goal — defer.** Named anti-pattern; infer from Level + Environment; ask after session one.
- **Seed Form — cut as a standalone gate.** A blob with no decision and no payoff; fold Presence into the logistical screens.
- **Sex — merge** into Level/Environment.
- **Building Your Path — cut.** A loading screen dressed as meaning.
- **Avatar Formation + Naming Ceremony — move AFTER first workout.** They land ten times harder as a reward than as a tax. Naming an unearned thing is hollow.
Leanest: `Signup → Environment → Schedule → Level/Sex → Home with first session queued`. Three or four screens, no goal, no naming, no blob-gazing. Ceremonies become Day-1-complete rewards.

## Peer review (anonymization: A=Expansionist, B=Contrarian, C=Executor, D=First Principles, E=Outsider)

**Reviewer 1** — Strongest: B (locates the contradiction at the doctrinal level — a witness made the embodiment of progression must also embody failure; "living mascot of backsliding at Home"). Biggest blind spot: A (assumes attachment is earnable before the product proves it won't shame; builds a 365-day thesis on a Day 0 the user never finishes). All missed: nobody proposed *testing* the Presence concept — no retention metric, A/B, or kill criterion; nobody asked whether removing the companion entirely beats fixing it.

**Reviewer 2** — Strongest: D (reframes to job-to-be-done and exposes the "Present, not Performing" vs ceremony contradiction; generative principle "least we can ask"). Biggest blind spot: A (misses the shame-carrying user; designs Day 90 for someone who won't survive Day 0). All missed: measurement / persona validation; and the assumption that ceremony and leanness are mutually exclusive — nobody asked whether the ceremony could be **skippable**, satisfying both attachment and "let me work out first."

**Reviewer 3** — Strongest: D (best diagnosis + generative principle; C is the best execution plan operationalizing it). Biggest blind spot: C (purely subtractive — relocates Avatar/Naming without costing what the cut does to attachment, the moat A identifies). All missed: **deferring everything quietly guts the Presence concept.** Synthesis nobody held: Presence is present from second zero but **un-ceremonied** — witnesses the logistics silently, and naming/avatar **emerge earned** from the first session as memory ("I've been here since before your first rep"). Keeps the lean gate AND the imprinting; the ceremony becomes retroactive, authored by behavior instead of demanded upfront.

## Chairman synthesis

**Verdict: C — rebuild the onboarding sequence, but preserve the Unified Presence concept.**

**Agreement (high confidence):** Goal must leave Day 0; Naming + Avatar Formation belong after the first workout as earned payoffs; Day 0 is logistics only (Environment, Schedule, Level + Sex merged); "Present, not Performing" is currently violated by the formation ceremony.

**Clash:** strip-to-logistics camp (Contrarian, Executor, Outsider) vs protect-the-imprinting camp (Expansionist). Peer review validated the Expansionist's blind spot but kept its core point.

**Blind spot caught:** deferring everything guts the Presence moat — resolved by the *present-but-un-ceremonied, naming-emerges-earned* synthesis. Plus: no measurement was proposed (D+1 return, A/B full-vs-lean, kill criteria, persona validation).

**Recommended structure**

Day 0 (logistics, Presence silent): `Signup → Presence appears (neutral, witnesses, asks nothing) → Environment → Schedule → Level + Sex → Home, first session queued`
Earned (after first workout): `Avatar takes form → Naming → Goal emerges (Presence earns the right to ask)`

**Keep:** Unified Presence concept; Environment/Schedule/Level; Seed Form as avatar origin; "Present, not Performing" (now honored).
**Change:** Goal → deferred & asked by Presence; Sex → merged into Level; Day-0 Presence → silent witness; jargon → plain language.
**Delete:** Seed Form as a staring gate; "Building Your Path"; the triple-ceremony stack on Day 0.

**Most risky decisions:** (1) opening with Goal (own anti-pattern); (2) Naming before first workout (checkout point); (3) Presence as "embodiment of progression" (also embodies failure for the lapsing user); (4) identical Seed Form vs "meeting you" claim; (5) no measurement / kill criteria / persona validation.

**One thing to do first:** pull Goal out of Day 0 and move the Naming Ceremony to after the first completed workout.
