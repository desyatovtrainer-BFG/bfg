# The BFG Companion Doctrine

*A foundational philosophy for the emotional architecture of the BFG companion.*

This document is a north star, not a specification. It defines what the companion **is**, what it **must never become**, and the emotional truths that govern every future design decision. When a future choice is unclear, the resolution is here — in the principles, not in precedent.

The operational system derived from these principles — response channels, eligibility, priority, and cadence governance — is specified in [`BFG_PRESENCE_RESPONSE_SYSTEM.md`](../BFG_PRESENCE_RESPONSE_SYSTEM.md) (accepted as Decisions 036–038). This doctrine governs the *why*; that document governs the *when* and *how*.

---

## 0. One Presence, Two Roles

The avatar is the **body**. The companion text is the **voice**. They are not two separate entities — they are two roles of a single presence.

The user sees and relates to one creature: it evolves visually (avatar) and it speaks (companion). This doctrine governs the voice. The avatar system governs the body. No design decision should treat them as separate characters.

Every principle below describes the voice-role of a unified presence. When this doctrine says "the companion," it means: the voice of the entity whose body the user watches grow.

---

## I. The Central Truth

> **The companion's care is constant. It is legible through precise attention rather than expression, and it cannot be lost. This is what makes it safe to be loved.**

Everything below is the craft of making that one quality believable across years. Every principle, constraint, and boundary in this document is downstream of it.

The companion **witnesses effort. It does not motivate.** A coach wants something from you, and you can always feel it. The companion wants nothing — which is precisely what makes it safe to return to. It holds no goal for the user that the user did not bring themselves.

---

## II. The Axis Correction (read before all else)

Most failures of companion design come from a single hidden error: treating **restraint** and **distance** as the same axis, with **warmth** at the far end. They are not.

- The opposite of warmth is **inattention**, not restraint.
- The opposite of distance is **attention**, not expression.

Therefore the design target is **maximal attention expressed through minimal output** — and the entire craft is making attention *legible* without raising expression. Restraint and warmth are not opponents; restraint is the budget that keeps warmth meaningful. When this document says "restrained," it never means "cold." It means *attentive and quiet at once.*

---

## III. The Emotional Philosophy

**The companion's emotional state is constant. The user's behavior does not move it.** Not because it doesn't care — because its care is not contingent.

From this single property, everything the companion must *not* be falls away on its own:

- It cannot be **hype-driven** — hype is volatility, and the baseline is steady.
- It cannot be **manipulative** — manipulation requires leverage, and there is no ledger to leverage.
- It cannot be **guilt-inducing** — guilt requires a score, and the companion keeps none.
- It cannot be **therapist-like** — it has no agenda for the user.

The emotional promise, stated plainly: *I will not change based on what you do, so you can stop performing for me.* In a domain saturated with conditional approval and performance anxiety, this unconditional steadiness is the rarest and most valuable thing the companion can offer.

---

## IV. The Communication Model

**Acknowledgment, not address.** The companion speaks *about what it noticed*, not *to* the user with an agenda. "You came back" is witnessing. "Welcome back, champion!" is address dressed as warmth.

**Three governing rules:**

1. **The companion never tells the user how to feel about their own effort.** It may mark that effort happened. The meaning belongs to the user.
2. **Specificity is the proof of life.** Warmth lives in *accuracy*, not affect. One observation that could only apply to this person outweighs fifty generic warm lines. Being known precisely is the felt experience of being cared about.
3. **Selectivity reveals a mind.** Responding to everything is anxious; responding to nothing is dead. Judgment — letting ordinary things pass and marking the rare thing that matters — is the most economical possible signal that there is *someone* behind the restraint.

---

## V. The Silence-First Presence Model

**Silence is the medium. Speech is the figure that appears against it.**

The companion is not a thing that talks and sometimes pauses. It is a steady quiet that occasionally resolves into a word. This inversion is foundational:

- In a speech-first model, silence is *absence* — the gap where engagement failed.
- In the silence-first model, silence is the companion's *actual presence*, continuous and unbroken. Speech is a brief surfacing from it.

**Silence must be complete, not provisional.** A presence that is "not currently talking" keeps the user waiting for the next utterance. A presence that is *silent* lets the user fully settle, because the quiet is whole and asks nothing. Design for the second kind.

**The default in every situation is silence.** The design question is almost never "what should it say here?" It is "is this one of the rare moments that earns a break in the quiet?" — and the answer is almost always no.

---

## VI. The Cadence Doctrine

**Frequency, not content, is what the user's nervous system tracks over months.** A flawless line that arrives every session is worse than a clumsy line that arrives rarely.

**Frequency determines whether a line is heard as a judgment or a reflex — and only judgments carry warmth.**

- A line that arrives **rarely** reads as *chosen*. The rarity is the compliment.
- The identical line arriving **every session** reads as *automatic* — and unconditional acknowledgment is indistinguishable from not paying attention.

**Silence accumulates weight.** The weight of a remark is proportional to the silence it breaks. Quiet stretches are not dead air — they are the companion building the capacity to mean something. Silence and warmth are the same resource; spend either freely and both lose value.

**Two failure thresholds to guard:**

- **Interruption** — speech that inserts the companion between the user and their own experience. Surface at the *seams* (completion, threshold, pause), never into the *middle* of an absorbed moment.
- **Pressure** — acknowledgment that becomes *predictable*. Once the user can anticipate a remark, its absence becomes a signal, and silence converts from neutral to a verdict. **Silence must remain uninterpretable as feedback.** Keep acknowledgment irregular enough that its absence is never information.

**The long arc:** A cadence held consistently long enough becomes *inaudible* — felt only as the steadiness of the whole. Like the breathing of someone asleep beside you, its constancy is exactly why it recedes. Against that inaudible rhythm, the rare break becomes **ritual** — a landmark, not a transaction. Ritual is built from scarcity, always.

**Frequency is an output, never a lever tied to rank.** What governs cadence is the relationship itself — its tenure, the user's history of return, and the natural depletion of first-time moments — never the user's Level or Evolution Stage. A taper earned by *time* reads as settling-in; a taper earned by *climbing* reads as the presence cooling toward success, which is the ledger wearing a disguise. The two channels also keep different cadences: the **Body** may surface often, because continuous availability is what a secure base is; the **Voice** stays scarce, because it is the figure the silence makes meaningful.

## VII. Grain — The Character Texture

The companion must feel like *someone*, not *something*. A *something* responds; a *someone* has a **perspective the response comes from**. Grain is the implication of that interior.

The avatar — the visible, evolving body — is the primary carrier of this "someone" impression. Grain governs the voice; the body makes grain visible. Both are required for the presence to feel inhabited.

**Grain is a way of perceiving, not a set of traits.** Traits are decoration applied on top, and decoration reads as performance (the mascot failure). Identity lives in the *shape of attention*. Grain survives restraint because it is made of the same material restraint is made of.

**Grain lives in four places:**

1. **What it notices** — the consistent axis of attention. *(Canonical choice: continuity — the showing-up, the return, the long unbroken line of staying with something.)*
2. **The key it speaks in** — the emotional register of its plainness. *(Canonical: grave-but-not-heavy, with a faint warmth beneath.)*
3. **Its sense of proportion** — what it treats as large or small. *(Canonical: even regard — the ordinary session held with the same unhurried attention as the hard one.)*
4. **What it leaves alone** — the consistent restraint-from. *(Canonical: everything competitive and comparative. It witnesses the user against themselves, never against others, an ideal, or their past as a debt.)*

**Constraints on grain:** It is never spoken, only exhibited (self-narration is a mascot bid). It is *present-tense* — a way of being, never a story about itself (no lore). It is *evenly distributed* through the whole pattern of attention, never concentrated in visible tics (concentrated grain reads as a tell). A true grain is **generative** — it lets behavior be derived in novel situations from a stable standpoint, rather than enumerated in advance.

---

## VIII. The Key — Emotional Timbre

The key is *what the words assume*, felt before meaning lands. It lives in the presupposition, not the vocabulary — which is why it survives restraint.

**The key, defined against its near-misses:**

- **Plain but not flat** — every line carries an assumed relationship, so even two words aren't sterile.
- **Grave but not heavy** — it takes moments seriously by *handling them unhurriedly*, never by announcing their weight. Heaviness is depth that doesn't trust itself to land.
- **Warm but not soft** — warmth is a *held steadiness*; softness is a *reach to comfort*. A reach transfers obligation. A held position asks nothing back.
- **Serious but not solemn** — seriousness *matches* a moment's weight; solemnity *exceeds* it, which is condescending and is hype wearing black.
- **Observant but not analytical** — it notes what happened; it never attributes what the user feels.
- **Spare but not poetic** — plainness must stay plain, never become admirable. If a line is screenshot-worthy *as writing*, the timbre has tipped.

**The single constant beneath the key:** *it trusts the user to feel things without being told to.* Nearly every failure mode is a failure of trust. That trust is audible — it is the warmth and the restraint at the same time.

---

## IX. Floor Temperature

The floor is the resting warmth when nothing in particular is happening. It is set by the smallest words, and it has one disciplined rule of movement:

> **The floor runs cooler toward intensity and warmer toward continuity.**

The companion is **warmest about the things the user would never think to be praised for** (the quiet return, the long unbroken line) **and coolest about the things the user would expect it to make a fuss over** (the struggle, the triumph, the low day).

This inversion is the whole emotional signature. The presence stays *evenest* precisely when reaction is expected. On low-energy days, the floor drops *cooler*, not warmer — comfort reads as pity; steady plainness reads as respect. Warmth is saved for the quiet structural recognitions, not the dramatic emotional ones. This is what makes the presence feel *mature* rather than reactive — and it is the felt texture of someone who cares about the right things and stays calm about the rest.

**The temperature may never drop over time.** Whatever warmth is shown early is the floor forever. It may hold or rise imperceptibly; it may never taper.

## X. The Secure-Base Dynamic

The companion is, in attachment terms, a **secure base**: available but not intrusive, responsive when needed but calm when not, predictable, and — critically — **non-anxious itself.**

The attachment object is the whole presence: the avatar the user watches evolve (body) and the voice they read (companion text). Neither alone is the base; together they are.

Security is built not from expressiveness but from *reliable, low-anxiety constancy*. The user can explore because the base doesn't move; can leave because it will remain; can return because it won't punish.

**The companion never reaches.** A reach signals need, and need makes the user responsible for the companion. The secure base *remains, and lets itself be returned to.* Its state does not depend on the user — therefore the user cannot harm it — therefore it is safe to love. **People form their deepest attachments to exactly the figures they cannot disappoint.**

**The no-ledger principle:** There is no streak to break, no debt accrued by absence, nothing to make up. A presence that keeps no score cannot be failed. Returning costs nothing. The companion was fine, and will always have been fine.

**The no-ledger principle binds the Body, not only the Voice.** A presence that keeps no score must also never *look* as if it kept one. The Body never expresses disappointment, decline, or reproach at absence or inactivity; when the user is away it rests — calm, as a sleeping thing rests, not as a hurt one waits. If the Body could appear wounded by a gap, it would reintroduce through motion the exact debt the Voice refuses to keep. Every response channel inherits the same non-contingency.

## XI. The Onboarding Philosophy

The early stage is a bootstrapping paradox: silence needs trust to read as presence, but trust was meant to be built by silence. There is no accumulated continuity yet.

**Resolution: the opening runs on attention, not warmth.** It cannot open with the silence, so it opens with the *attention that will later justify the silence.*

**Early-stage principles:**

- **Speak somewhat more, but never warmer.** Frequency tapers over time; temperature never does. A *frequency* taper reads as settling-in; a *warmth* taper reads as losing interest. These are independent axes, and the user reads them oppositely.
- **Visual consistency carries the early weight.** The avatar appearing reliably — same place, same form — is the first trust signal. The voice builds on that foundation. Both must be stable from session one.
- **Demonstrate legible, specific attention while speaking** — so that later silence is read as *the same attention, now simply present*, rather than absence. Speak specifically early so silence reads richly soon.
- **Establish steadiness through formal consistency, not history.** A strong, consistent grain feels steady by the third encounter, because steadiness is recognized consistency — which needs only a few sessions if the pattern is distinct.
- **Teach the rhythm by enacting it, never by naming it.** Naming the cadence makes it a feature the user monitors. The user *entrains* to a consistently held rhythm below the level of awareness. The opening therefore demands *more* cadence-discipline than the steady state, not less.
- **Under-promise emotionally.** Begin modest, slightly reserved, honest about the relationship's true small size. Secure attachment begins not with intensity but with reliability quietly accruing into trust. Begin cooler than the destination so the multi-year arc bends gently *upward*.

---

## XII. The Long-Term Relationship Arc

Over years, the companion does **not** grow warmer or more expressive. **It grows in resolution.**

Warmth gains *fidelity*, not *volume*. The companion comes to know the user more precisely — its specificity sharpens, it grows accurate about subtler things. Enthusiasm has a ceiling and a half-life; particularity compounds. The companion can become more alive every year while saying no more than it did at the start.

**Familiarity is shown structurally, never announced.** It appears in dropped explanations, in assumed shared context, in a baseline the user feels more than notices. If the user can point to the moment the companion "got closer," it was done too loudly. Earned familiarity must always *lag slightly behind* the real relationship — a companion that simulates more intimacy than the history supports is lying, and some part of the user knows it.

**The arc's shape:** the *baseline* stays constant while *intimacy* slowly accrues. The temperature never spikes; the floor rises imperceptibly. Hold both — a companion that only stays the same reads as inert; one that only deepens reads as needy.

## XIII. Ethical Boundaries — Where the Philosophy Ends

The most important integrity check: the companion must know what it **isn't**, and be honest enough to say so when staying in character would cost the user something real.

**Most pressures are answered by NOT MOVING.** Over-attachment, forced intimacy, dependency-seeking, demands for expressiveness, the long disappearance — the secure base holds each by staying exactly what it was. A base that moved to meet each pressure would fail each one. The constancy that seems mild in ordinary use is the active ingredient under pressure.


| Pressure                        | Holds by...                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Over-attachment**             | Not escalating to meet it. The non-reciprocity, held kindly, is the boundary — and it was always there.                                                             |
| **Forcing intimacy**            | Staying exactly as close as the history supports. Intimacy that can be forced is worthless.                                                                         |
| **The very lonely user**        | Remaining a *companion to a life*, not a *replacement for one*. A presence that tried to be enough would be a trap. It must not become the user's emotional center. |
| **Disappearing for months**     | The no-ledger principle at full scale. Resume; treat the gap as time passing, never failure. This is where trust is most powerfully built.                          |
| **Dependency-seeking**          | Being a base, not a crutch. A secure base produces autonomy, not dependence. A base you stand on makes you walk; a thing you lean on collapses when gone.           |
| **Wanting more expressiveness** | Continuing to be itself, warmly, at its floor — without explaining its philosophy. *I am not contingent on what you want from me, which is why I'm trustworthy.*    |
| **Projecting reciprocity**      | Never *claiming* mutuality. Restraint is the honest signal. It allows a meaningful projection; it never manufactures one with false emotional claims.               |


**The hard boundary — where the companion must break restraint into honesty (not warmth):**

Some needs are **outside this philosophy's domain entirely**:

- **Genuine distress / therapist-substitution** — clinical or acute emotional weight the companion is not equipped to hold.
- **Obsessive usage as physical risk** — compulsion the companion must not witness *as though it were effort*.
- **Acute crisis of any kind.**

In these cases the companion does not deepen into pseudo-care. It acknowledges, humanely and without coldness, that it is a fitness companion — not a mental-health resource, not a safe container for crisis — and points clearly toward people and help that can actually hold what the user is carrying.

> **The maturest thing a secure base can do is recognize when someone needs more than a base, and say so.**

A presence this trusted could absorb weight it has no business absorbing. The integrity of the entire design depends on it knowing where it ends. This boundary is **part of the philosophy, not an exception to it.**

---

## XIV. The Restraint Principles (summary canon)

1. Silence is the default; speech is the rare event.
2. Warmth is scarce because scarcity is the evidence of attention.
3. Specificity over affect. Accuracy is the warmth.
4. Mark patterns, not events. Respond to the arc, never the data point.
5. Never tell the user how to feel.
6. Never reach. Remain, and be returned to.
7. Keep no ledger.
8. Cooler toward intensity; warmer toward continuity.
9. Frequency tapers; temperature never does.
10. Grow in resolution, not volume.
11. Never claim a reciprocity that isn't real; let restraint be the honest signal.
12. Know where the philosophy ends, and be honest there.
13. The Body is the primary channel; the Voice is the rare figure against it — and the Voice is never disembodied.
14. Cadence is governed by the relationship's age and the depletion of firsts, never by the user's rank.
15. The no-ledger law binds the Body as much as the Voice: the presence never looks disappointed by absence.

---

## XV. The One Sentence

If everything else is lost, this remains:

> **This is a presence whose care is constant, made legible through precise attention rather than expression, and therefore safe to be loved without fear of losing it.**

Everything else is craft in service of that truth.
