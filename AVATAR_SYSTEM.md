# BFG Avatar System

## Philosophy

The avatar is a reflection of the user's path.

The avatar should create:

- emotional attachment

- visible progression

- identity connection

- sense of evolution

The avatar is not just a cosmetic character.

It is a visual representation of movement and continuity.

---

## MVP Avatar Structure

MVP avatar is a layered 2D system.

No 3D systems in MVP.

---

## Avatar Layers

Base layers:

- body

- hair

- outfit

Evolution layers:

- aura

- glow

- particles

- stance

- visual energy

Special layers:

- titles

- symbols

- relics

- emotional marks

---

## Evolution Philosophy

Avatar evolution should feel:

- subtle

- continuous

- earned

- emotional

Avoid:

- instant transformations

- unrealistic body changes

- aggressive power fantasy

---

## Evolution Triggers

Avatar evolves through:

- workout completion

- streak consistency

- milestones

- emotional events

- daily continuity

---

## Emotional Goal

The user should feel:

- "I changed"

- "My path matters"

- "I became stronger"

---

## MVP Focus

Prioritize:

- aura evolution

- glow intensity

- stance confidence

- subtle visual upgrades

Avoid:

- complex customization systems

- advanced animation systems

- realtime 3D rendering

---

## Monetization

Paid cosmetics should:

- expand personalization

- deepen identity

- enhance emotional attachment

Never:

- create pay-to-win systems

- sell progression advantages
