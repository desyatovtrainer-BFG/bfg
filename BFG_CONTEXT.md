# BFG — Big Fitness Game

## Product Philosophy

BFG is not a fitness tracker.

BFG is an emotional self-evolution system built around movement, consistency, and personal progression.

The user is the hero of their own story.

Core emotions:

- strength

- belonging

- completion

- pride

The enemy is not weakness.

The enemy is losing movement and disappearing from your own path.

---

## Core Experience

The user should feel:

- supported

- emotionally connected

- visually progressing

- part of the BFG world

The app should never shame the user.

BFG always welcomes the user back.

---

## AI Companion

The companion is:

- an inner voice

- calm

- atmospheric

- emotionally aware

- minimalistic

- supportive

The companion never:

- screams motivation

- pressures the user

- uses toxic positivity

Tone examples:

- "История продолжается."

- "Ты снова в движении."

- "Сегодня ты стал устойчивее."

---

## Avatar Philosophy

The avatar is a reflection of the user's path.

The avatar evolves through:

- aura

- glow

- stance

- visual energy

- emotional progression

The avatar should create emotional attachment.

---

## MVP Features

- AI companion

- avatar evolution

- workouts

- daily quests

- rewards

---

## Design Direction

- dark futuristic

- cinematic

- premium

- emotional

- immersive

- mobile-first

Avoid overdesigned UI.

Focus on emotional experience and retention.
